Comparison rules

Conservative, exact comparisons

  • Institutional student ID is the lookup key; name alone never identifies a student.
  • Name, course, semester, and email are compared when both sides have values.
  • Missing fields, duplicate source identifiers, or pending duplicate candidates require review.
  • No student record is merged or changed by a comparison.
