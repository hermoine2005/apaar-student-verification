APAAR Verify user guide

Practical guidance for institution administrators and workspace users.

All users

  1. Sign in with your assigned username and password.
  2. Enter the one-time code sent to your verified email address.
  3. Use Students to search and open records within your institution.
  4. Review imports and validation messages before confirming any changes.
  5. Use Account security to verify your email and support password recovery.
  6. Sign out when finished, especially on shared computers.

Institution administrators

  1. Open Users & access to create accounts for your institution.
  2. Provide a valid email address; users need it for OTP and password recovery.
  3. Assign only the role needed for each person's work.
  4. Deactivate accounts promptly when access is no longer required.
  5. Review Audit history for traceability of important actions.

Administrators cannot grant administrator privileges from the standard account creation form.

Role overview

  • Institution administrator: manages institution accounts and workspace configuration.
  • Operator: maintains student records and imports.
  • Verifier: reviews records and verification cases.
  • Auditor: reviews records and audit history without routine editing access.

Permissions are enforced on server routes; hiding a menu item alone is not access control.

Data and verification limits

  • Use synthetic or authorized test data in this prototype.
  • CSV imports should be checked for errors and duplicate records before confirmation.
  • Source dataset comparisons are institution-provided checks, not official government verification.
  • This application does not connect to APAAR, ABC or DigiLocker services unless separately integrated and authorized.

Account recovery

Use “Forgot password?” on the sign-in page. Reset links are sent only to verified account email addresses and expire after 30 minutes. If delivery fails, contact the administrator.

Configure SMTP before production use. In local development, auth codes may be logged to the application console and should never be exposed in a public deployment.

Administrator checklist

  • Set a strong private SECRET_KEY and admin password.
  • Configure SMTP credentials using environment variables.
  • Use HTTPS and set COOKIE_SECURE=1 in production.
  • Back up the database before upgrades and test restore procedures.
  • Never commit .env, credentials or real student data.
